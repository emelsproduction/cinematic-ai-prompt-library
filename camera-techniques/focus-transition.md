# Focus transition

**Use for:** video · **Tags:** rack-focus, depth-of-field, composition

## Template prompt

Compose `[foreground subject/object]` softly out of focus in the near left of frame, with `[background subject/object]` clearly visible at `[distance]`. Hold the camera still. Slowly shift focus from the foreground to the background over `[duration]`, keeping both objects and their positions fixed. Use believable shallow depth of field, natural focus breathing kept subtle, and consistent exposure and lighting. No camera movement, no object movement, no cuts, no new elements, no abrupt focus snap.

## Still-image adaptation

For a still, choose either the foreground or background as the sharp focal plane and describe the other as softly defocused.

## Review

Confirm the focus shift is gradual and does not alter object shape, position, or illumination.
