# Controlled push-in

**Use for:** video · **Tags:** dolly-in, emphasis, camera-movement

## Template prompt

Begin on a `[wide / medium]` view of `[subject and environment]`. Make one slow, smooth forward camera move that ends on a `[medium / close]` view of `[specific focal detail]`. Keep the subject's position and appearance stable, maintain a level horizon, and preserve the original light direction and background layout. The subject performs only `[small action]`; the camera movement provides the emphasis. Continuous shot, constant pace, no zoom, no cuts, no orbit, no sudden acceleration.

## Timing guide

For a short clip, reserve the first moment for a stable opening, move evenly through the middle, and settle briefly at the end. Adjust duration to the tool's supported range.

## Review

Look for unintended zooming, background warping, or subject scale changes that do not match a forward camera move.
