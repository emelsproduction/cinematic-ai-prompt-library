# Lens and framing

## Common Cinematic Lens Guide

- 24mm: wide, immersive, dramatic environment framing
- 35mm: natural wide composition with context
- 50mm: classic neutral perspective, strong character focus
- 85mm: portrait compression, shallow depth, intimate close-ups
- 135mm: compressed, cinematic, dramatic compression

## Framing language

- close-up: head and shoulders, emotional emphasis
- medium shot: subject + environment context
- wide shot: full scene and spatial storytelling
- over-the-shoulder: perspective-driven narrative tension

## Prompt examples

- 35mm wide establishing shot, cinematic architecture, natural perspective
- 85mm tight close-up, shallow depth of field, dramatic subject isolation
