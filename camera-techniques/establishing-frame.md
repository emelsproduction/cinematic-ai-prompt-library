# Establishing-frame composition

**Use for:** image or video · **Tags:** wide-shot, composition, establishing, depth

## Template prompt

A cinematic establishing frame of `[location]` at `[time/weather]`. Place `[primary subject]` at `[position in frame]` and use `[foreground element]` to create depth; retain clear environmental context in the background. The visual path leads from `[foreground detail]` toward `[focal point]`. Use a `[wide / medium-wide]` composition with natural perspective, a level horizon, readable subject silhouette, and balanced negative space. Light comes from `[direction/source]`; maintain consistent shadows and realistic scale. No unnecessary foreground clutter, no accidental text or watermark.

## Camera notes

Choose one: eye-level for natural observation; low angle for scale; elevated angle for spatial clarity. For video, begin and end on a stable composition before adding motion.
