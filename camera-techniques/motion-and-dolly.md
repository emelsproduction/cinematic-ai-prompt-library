# Motion and dolly technique notes

## Camera movement ideas

- slow dolly-in: adds tension and focus
- crane rise: reveals scale and atmosphere
- handheld drift: adds realism and urgency
- orbit shot: dynamic subject reveal
- static frame: creates stillness, gravity, or unease

## Prompt examples

- slow dolly-in, moody lighting, subtle motion blur, cinematic realism
- handheld tracking shot through rain and neon reflections

## Best practices

Focus on one motion principle at a time. Motion works best when the subject, lighting, and framing support a single emotional beat.
