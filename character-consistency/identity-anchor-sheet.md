# Identity anchor sheet

Use this sheet to define a fictional or consented character consistently. Avoid relying on vague descriptors alone; record a small number of visually distinctive, stable traits.

## Character record

- Name or ID: `[name]`
- Approximate adult age: `[age range]`
- Face shape and features: `[distinctive but concise details]`
- Hair: `[color, length, style]`
- Eyes: `[color and notable shape, if relevant]`
- Skin tone: `[description]`
- Build and posture: `[description]`
- Signature clothing: `[garment, cut, color, material]`
- Stable accessory: `[one item or none]`
- Reference asset: `[filename or approved link]`

## Base prompt

A consistent portrait of `[name/ID]`, an adult with `[face and hair anchors]`, `[eye and skin-tone anchors]`, wearing `[signature clothing]`, with `[one distinctive feature]`. Preserve the same facial proportions, hairline, hairstyle, and accessory as the approved reference. Neutral expression, simple uncluttered background, soft even light, eye-level camera, realistic anatomy and skin texture. Do not introduce additional accessories or alter identity-defining traits.
