# Wardrobe continuity sequence

## Goal

Keep one character's outfit stable across a sequence of distinct shots.

## Fixed character and wardrobe block

`[Name/ID]`, adult, `[identity anchors]`, wearing the same `[garment type, color, cut, material]`, `[footwear]`, and `[single accessory]` in every shot. No wardrobe changes, added layers, color shifts, or accessory substitutions.

## Shot prompts

1. **Establishing:** full figure in `[location]`, standing still, wide shot, soft natural light.
2. **Action:** same outfit and location, `[simple action]`, medium shot, consistent light direction.
3. **Close detail:** same outfit, close view of `[garment/accessory detail]`, neutral background.

## Continuity notes

Use the same reference image and generation settings when supported. Review garment seams, buttons, patterns, and accessory placement. If the tool cannot preserve details reliably, select and edit outputs rather than implying exact continuity.
