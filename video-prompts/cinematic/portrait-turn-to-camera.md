# Portrait turn to camera

**Type:** video · **Tags:** cinematic, portrait, character, slow-motion, studio

## Prompt

A person in a dark tailored jacket stands beside a softly lit window in a quiet studio. In a single continuous medium close-up, they slowly turn their gaze from the window toward the camera, then hold a calm, thoughtful expression. The camera remains locked off; only the head and eyes move subtly. Soft side light, natural skin detail, shallow depth of field, restrained film grain, realistic timing and anatomy. Preserve facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, and background exactly; no speaking, no extra gestures, no camera zoom, no cuts, no text.

## Shot plan

4–5 seconds · locked medium close-up · one slow gaze turn · short still hold at end.

## Review checklist

Check identity stability across frames, natural eye movement, and absence of unrequested mouth or body motion.
