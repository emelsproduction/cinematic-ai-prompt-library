# Cinematic video prompts

## Example: slow-burn opening shot

### Prompt

Slow cinematic opening shot, a lone figure walks through a rain-soaked neon street at night, reflections on wet pavement, subtle camera drift, atmospheric fog, moody blue and magenta lighting, realistic motion blur, wide lens, cinematic composition, dramatic tension, subtle handheld realism, ultra-detailed environment.

### Suggested settings

- Model: Runway, Kling, or Pika
- Duration: 5-10 seconds
- Camera: handheld wide shot with subtle drift
- Style: noir cinematic realism
