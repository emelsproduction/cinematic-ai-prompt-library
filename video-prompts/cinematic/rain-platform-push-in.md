# Rain-platform push-in

**Type:** video · **Tags:** cinematic, rain, dolly-in, night, atmosphere

## Prompt

A lone commuter waits beneath the canopy of a nearly empty station at night as rain falls beyond the platform. In one continuous shot, the camera makes a very slow, stable push from a wide view to a medium-wide view; the commuter remains still, then glances toward a distant arriving light. Rain and reflections move naturally, distant lamps shimmer subtly, and the subject's coat shifts slightly in the breeze. Cool blue ambient light with warm practical lamps, realistic motion, restrained film grain, grounded cinematic realism. Keep the person's appearance, clothing, and position stable; no cuts, no sudden zoom, no new people, no readable signs.

## Shot plan

5–7 seconds · wide to medium-wide · one slow forward move · subject movement limited to a single glance.

## Review checklist

Check camera stability, consistent identity, natural rain direction, and that the light source does not jump or multiply.
