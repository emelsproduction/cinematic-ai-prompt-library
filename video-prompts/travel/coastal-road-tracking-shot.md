# Coastal road tracking shot

**Type:** video · **Tags:** travel, tracking, coast, daylight, cinematic

## Prompt

A dark vintage station wagon drives at a steady moderate speed along a quiet coastal road, ocean visible beyond the guardrail. Track parallel to the car from a stable vehicle-mounted camera; keep the car centered while foreground grasses pass smoothly with believable parallax. Overcast late-afternoon light, soft sea haze, natural reflections on the bodywork, muted blue-gray palette. Maintain the same vehicle shape and direction throughout one continuous shot, realistic wheel rotation and road motion, no passing traffic, no cuts, no sudden camera acceleration, no text or logos.

## Shot plan

6 seconds · medium-wide side tracking · constant speed · level horizon.

## Review checklist

Check that wheels rotate naturally, the car design stays consistent, and background parallax matches the camera motion.
