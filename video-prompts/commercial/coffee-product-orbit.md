# Coffee product orbit

**Type:** video · **Tags:** commercial, product, orbit, studio, macro

## Prompt

A ceramic cup of black coffee sits on a walnut table beside a folded linen napkin. The camera performs a slow quarter-circle orbit around the cup while keeping its rim in focus; a thin ribbon of steam rises gently and dissipates. Warm window-like key light creates a soft highlight on the ceramic and a believable shadow on the table. Preserve the cup shape, coffee level, and object positions throughout. Premium but natural product film, shallow depth of field, smooth motion, one uninterrupted take, no hands entering, no added props, no text or logo.

## Shot plan

5 seconds · slow 90-degree orbit · cup remains the focal point · subtle steam only.

## Review checklist

Check object stability, coherent orbit perspective, steam direction, and consistent cup rim geometry.
