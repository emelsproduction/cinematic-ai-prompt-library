# Forest mist reveal

**Type:** video · **Tags:** fantasy, forest, reveal, crane, atmosphere

## Prompt

At dawn in an old cedar forest, the camera slowly rises from a carpet of ferns to reveal a small stone footbridge across a stream. Ground mist drifts gently between tree trunks; the stream flows steadily and leaves move only in a light breeze. Keep the trees, bridge, and stream layout fixed as the camera rises on a smooth vertical crane move. Cool dawn light with a small warm opening in the distance, richly detailed natural textures, quiet grounded fantasy mood, continuous shot, no creatures appearing, no fast movement, no scene transitions.

## Shot plan

6–8 seconds · low angle to eye-level reveal · slow vertical rise · environmental motion kept subtle.

## Review checklist

Check that the bridge and tree layout do not morph and the camera path remains a single smooth rise.
