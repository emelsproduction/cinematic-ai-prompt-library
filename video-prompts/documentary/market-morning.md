# Market morning observation

**Type:** video · **Tags:** documentary, market, handheld, morning, people

## Prompt

A small neighborhood produce market opens in the early morning. A vendor arranges oranges while two shoppers pass behind, naturally crossing the frame without acknowledging the camera. Use a gentle handheld observational shot with small human movement, not exaggerated shake; hold on the vendor and fruit while the background stays active. Soft overcast daylight, authentic street color, realistic gestures and pace, documentary tone. Keep faces and clothing stable during the shot, avoid duplicated people or objects, no sudden camera pans, no legible signs, no captions.

## Shot plan

6 seconds · medium shot · subtle handheld drift · one clear foreground action.

## Review checklist

Check natural hands and walking, stable identities, non-repeating background activity, and restrained camera movement.
