# Hero portrait

## Prompt

A cinematic hero portrait of a confident young woman in a dark studio, wearing a tailored black blazer and silver jewelry, dramatic side lighting, soft volumetric haze, ultra-detailed skin texture, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, high contrast, realistic film grain, polished commercial portrait, elegant composition, moody atmosphere.

## Negative prompt

blurry, low detail, distorted face, extra limbs, oversaturated colors, flat lighting, text, watermark

## Notes

This prompt works especially well for luxury branding, editorial portraiture, or a character introduction in a visual story. It pairs well with a clean background and controlled rim light for more dramatic separation.
