# Rain-soaked arrival

**Type:** image · **Tags:** cinematic, noir, night, rain, wide-shot

## Prompt

A solitary traveler in a charcoal coat arrives at a nearly empty train platform after midnight, viewed from across the tracks. Rain stipples the glass canopy and catches the amber station lamps; a distant train is only a soft band of light in the background. Compose the traveler in the right third with generous negative space, wet reflections leading toward them, restrained teal-and-amber palette, realistic fabric and pavement texture, subtle 35mm film grain, cinematic still, natural perspective, no readable signage.
