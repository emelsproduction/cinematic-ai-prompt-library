# Luxury fragrance campaign

**Type:** image · **Tags:** product, luxury, studio, commercial, macro-detail

## Prompt

A single sculptural fragrance bottle of `[shape and material]` on a dark travertine plinth, with a folded translucent fabric backdrop. One large soft key light creates a clean highlight along the glass edge; a narrow warm rim separates the bottle from the background. Label area is blank and unobstructed, cap and liquid proportions are physically plausible, reflections are controlled, composition is sparse with intentional copy space on the left. Premium beauty campaign still, realistic glass and stone, precise product focus, no invented lettering, no extra objects, no watermark.

## Adapt

Bottle silhouette: `[description]` · liquid tint: `[color]` · backdrop: `[material/color]` · campaign mood: `[quiet / bold / botanical]`.

## Suggested setup

Still image · 4:5 or 1:1 · product at right third · softbox key and restrained rim light.

## Review checklist

Verify the bottle silhouette, cap, reflections, and blank label. Add final typography separately in design software.
