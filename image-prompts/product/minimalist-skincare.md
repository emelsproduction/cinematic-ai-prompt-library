# Minimalist skincare still

**Type:** image · **Tags:** product, skincare, minimalist, commercial, studio

## Prompt

A single matte-white skincare jar beside a shallow pool of clear water on a pale limestone surface. A soft overhead source and white bounce create gentle gradients, a precise contact shadow, and clean reflections without glare. One translucent leaf enters the frame as a subtle organic accent; ample uncluttered negative space surrounds the product. Contemporary, calm skincare campaign, realistic materials, centered label panel left blank, crisp product edges, no extra packaging, no lettering, no watermark.

## Adapt

Container: `[jar / bottle / tube]` · surface: `[limestone / ceramic / glass]` · accent: `[leaf / folded cotton / none]`.

## Suggested setup

Still image · 1:1 or 4:5 · soft top key and fill · product label added in post.

## Review checklist

Check that the container remains a single consistent object and that the blank label is clean enough for later design.
