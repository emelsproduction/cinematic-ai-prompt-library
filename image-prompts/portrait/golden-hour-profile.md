# Golden-hour profile

**Type:** image · **Tags:** portrait, editorial, golden-hour, natural-light

## Prompt

Editorial portrait of `[person]` at `[location]` just before sunset, shown from the shoulders up in a relaxed three-quarter profile. Warm low sunlight rims the hair and cheek; soft open-sky fill preserves natural shadow detail. Keep skin texture believable, expression unforced, background softly recognizable rather than abstract, and color grading warm but restrained. 85mm portrait perspective, shallow but usable depth of field, fine film grain, intimate cinematic realism, no beauty-filter look, no text or watermark.

## Adapt

Set age-appropriate appearance, hairstyle, wardrobe, environment, and emotional tone. Change only one or two of these between variations.

## Suggested setup

Still image · 4:5 portrait crop · eye-level camera · single warm key with soft ambient fill.

## Review checklist

Check facial anatomy, natural skin detail, catchlights, and that the background does not compete with the face.
