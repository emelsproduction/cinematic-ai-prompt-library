# Desert wayfinder

**Type:** image · **Tags:** fantasy, character, desert, epic, environment

## Prompt

A lone wayfinder in layered indigo travel clothes stands on a ridge above a vast salt desert at dawn, holding a small brass navigation instrument. Wind lifts only the loose edges of the scarf; the pose is calm and purposeful rather than heroic. Pale rose light meets cool blue shadows, distant rock formations fade into atmospheric haze, and fine salt patterns lead the eye toward the figure. Wide cinematic composition, grounded material detail, coherent anatomy, restrained fantasy realism, no glowing effects, no text or symbols.
